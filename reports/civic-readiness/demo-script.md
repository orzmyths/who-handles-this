# 90-second prototype demonstration

All reports below are synthetic. Do not use actual personal identifiers or addresses.

## Prepare before presenting

- Use the existing passcode without showing it to the audience.
- Leave one previously created, appropriate linked review report available in the staff queue; note its report/case links.
- Use English narration or set the Spanish interface. Translation may take time; navigation, phone and source links remain independently usable.
- Test live availability before presenting. If AI is unavailable, disclose the outage and show clearly dated saved results rather than pretending they are a new live run.

## 0–15 seconds: The resident's words

Enter: **I received a letter about my unemployment benefits and my trash bill.**

Or use Spanish: **Recibí una carta sobre mis beneficios de desempleo y mi factura de basura.**

Say: “This resident has two needs, but doesn't need to know the office names or which level of government handles them.”

## 15–35 seconds: Meaningful AI and bounded recommendations

Show the separate EDD and San José Environmental Services recommendations and service/action evidence.

Say: “AI extracts the services from the language. The supporting text is kept in the resident's own words. Rules constrain the office choices and check the evidence; they do not prove that every interpretation is correct.”

Open one official source. Show its jurisdiction, reviewed evidence and appropriate contact purpose.

## 35–50 seconds: Resident-controlled handoff

Show the departmental draft, including its review warnings and approval checks.

Say: “The resident checks the draft and opens it in their own email app. This prototype doesn't send email or submit an official case.”

Do not claim delivery. Do not send the demonstration email.

## 50–75 seconds: Human review is connected

Open the prepared linked case in the simulated staff workspace.

Use an appropriate supported agency, add a clear demo reason and approve it; do not approve an intentionally incorrect office just to make the demo look successful.

Follow the resident-report link and refresh if needed. Show the reviewed decision separately from the original automated result.

Say: “This demonstrates the review loop. No real City staff are monitoring the prototype.”

## 75–90 seconds: Evidence and responsible limits

Show the saved exact German expired-license failure and the retest results in the readiness report.

Say: “Testing caught unnecessary ambiguity for a German report and a non-verbatim quote in a combined report. We improved extraction without relaxing evidence checks. These are small synthetic regression tests, not a production accuracy claim. Before deployment, the City must approve identity, access, retention, language quality and integration.”
